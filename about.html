Background

About me

I'm a climate hazard consultant and geo-data specialist working at the intersection of flood risk modelling, anticipatory action, and disaster risk reduction. My work is driven by a conviction that good spatial data — made accessible and legible — is one of the most effective tools we have for protecting communities before climate hazards become disasters.

I care deeply about two things that are often treated as separate: technical rigour and genuine accessibility. Too much of the knowledge produced in climate science stays locked behind academic language or expensive software. I try to build tools, tutorials, and outputs that bridge that gap — so that communities, governments, and humanitarian actors anywhere in the world can make informed decisions with data that was built for them, not just about them.

Approach

I work across the full project lifecycle — from defining data requirements and building processing pipelines, to producing deliverables and presenting findings to non-technical stakeholders. I'm comfortable in the command line, in the field, and in a client meeting.

My technical work centres on open-source tools and reproducible workflows. I believe that if your method can't be explained, it probably shouldn't be trusted — so transparency and documentation are non-negotiable parts of how I build.
