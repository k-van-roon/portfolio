2 · chicago → enschede

I moved from Chicago to the Netherlands in August 2019, which is crazy given how long it's been! I moved into a student house with seven other students from my bachelor's program. I attended the honours college at the University of Twente, which is located in Enschede. Enschede is on the eastern border with Germany, so despite being in the Netherlands, for a good chunk of my time I was actually 2–3 hours from any big, notable city like Amsterdam.

But Enschede is great! Lots of activities and international students.

3 · year one (and a very long weekend)

My first year was really nice! Made lots of friends, tried lots of new things, and stuck with some old things (soccer). Midway through March, all our classes were canceled on a Thursday, so I decided to go home for the weekend to visit my parents in Amsterdam (they moved to NL in autumn 2019). That ended up being sort of silly because the weekend turned into almost half a year at home!

4 · the house bubble

I went back to university and lived in a new house with my best girl friends, and this was truly the most fun I had during my time at university. The pandemic raged on, but we managed to have lots of fun in our little house bubble.

Papi, the house dog!

They're still my best friends to this day, and the last time we all met up was in 2025 for Kirsten's graduation. It is super strange that we all used to live together, because everyone is now spread out across different disciplines and continents.

the girlies, all together again
5 · hengelo & finishing my bachelor's

For my third and final year of my bachelor's, I moved into my first apartment all to myself in the city next to Enschede, Hengelo. I commuted to my classes by bike, which usually took fifteen to forty-five minutes, depending on which building I needed to go to. I was supposed to study abroad in Prague for a semester (but don't worry, I went there with the girlies just after my BSc), but it ended up getting canceled because of the pandemic. I managed to still have fun stuck at home, though! Some friends started a dinner club where each person cooked once a week, so we saw a lot of each other.

I did a minor in Earth Observation and one in Multifunctional Flood Defenses, and then I wrote my thesis on innovations in flash flood forecasting. I graduated in 2022 with a degree in Technology & Liberal Arts and Sciences, with a specialization in civil engineering and earth observation.

BSc graduation!
and Prague with the girlies, finally!
6 · two more years (a master's!)

I absolutely could not see myself starting to work after finishing my bachelor's, so I decided to do a master's that started that autumn, in Geospatial Engineering, still at the University of Twente. Staying in Enschede for an extra two years was great, and I had loads of fun times with friends!

I graduated with my master's, specializing in Natural Hazard Risk Reduction, in 2024.

MSc graduation!
7 · highlight: fieldwork in dominica

I spent my final master's year working on my thesis, investigating probabilistic weather forecasting for climate resilience for the island of Dominica. This was a super cool project, also because my department paid for me to go do fieldwork on the island! It also paved the way for the work I would end up doing in my career.

8 · finally out of the east: delft

Leaving university, I was really well trained in modelling and predicting natural hazard risk, and this was basically my dream: getting to work on natural disaster prevention and getting to investigate extreme weather events.

I did a brief stint of work in Delft at Deltares, the water institute of the Netherlands, and living in Delft was great because it was so much closer to the “happening” part of NL.

Unfortunately, the organization went through a reorganization and I was let go, which was a bummer, but who knows, I could see myself ending up there later in life sometime :)

9 · what i do now

I found my current job in the funniest way. One of my old professors, who I did my master's research with, worked at this little consultancy company based smack dab in the middle of the Netherlands, and lo and behold, their whole focus is reducing risk from climate-induced natural hazards.

I started working there as a climate hazard consultant at the beginning of 2025, and it has been a blast! I get to model flooding almost every day, and I am also super involved in the humanitarian sector, which is something I value so much. During peak seasons, like now during hurricane season, we often get emergency activations from the United Nations, where I estimate where a flood or landslide is likely to occur and what kinds of damages will follow (exposed populations and monetary damages). It's activities like these where I feel very fulfilled, like I am actually contributing to society, and that's nice, of course.

10 · life these days

Overall, I am still really enjoying my time in the Netherlands, and certainly cannot see myself moving back to the US anytime soon. I've built a really rich community of friends and hobbies, and I also really love the work-life balance and lifestyle here.

🚲 biking

I LOVE biking; I cannot imagine not being able to bike or walk everywhere.

🏃 running

Seven years on, I have grown up a lot and found new things that excite me. I love going on runs in the farmlands outside cities (I know this is very stereotypical for mid-twenties, but I cannot help it).

🧵 sewing & embroidery

I have also gotten really handy with clothes and personalization, so my sewing machine and embroidery supplies have definitely been large purchases over the years.

🍞 cooking & baking

Somewhere during the pandemic, I also got really into cooking! I really love being able to source all the ingredients locally and build things up. And some things never change: I'm still baking loads.

✈️ travel

I was never big on travel as a kid (even though we did it all the time), but I have slowly but surely figured out the perfect Katherine formula for travel, and it, more often than not, includes baked goods, taking naps outdoors, wine, and visiting places with friends who are locals ;)

🎲 board games & football

I still love board games with the fam, and football-ing.
