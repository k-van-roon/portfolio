Background

About me

I'm a climate hazard consultant and geo-data specialist with expertise in flood risk modelling, anticipatory action, and disaster risk reduction. I've delivered operational flood analysis and information products for government and humanitarian clients across Canada, the Caribbean, and the Mediterranean — from near-real-time emergency response to long-term national risk assessment.

My MSc research at the University of Twente focused on integrating probabilistic weather forecasting into flood early warning systems for Dominica — a small island developing state acutely exposed to tropical cyclones and flash flooding. That work sits at the heart of what drives me: getting the right information to the right people before a hazard becomes a disaster.

I care about two things that are often treated as separate: technical rigour and genuine accessibility. Too much climate science stays locked behind academic language or expensive proprietary tools. I build workflows, tutorials, and products designed to bridge that gap — so that practitioners and communities anywhere in the world can act on spatial data that was made for them, not just about them.

Approach

I work across the full project lifecycle — from scoping data requirements and building processing pipelines, to producing deliverables and presenting findings to non-technical stakeholders. I've coordinated multidisciplinary teams under genuinely difficult conditions: emergency flood response, complex multi-partner consortia, and projects where the operational stakes are high.

My technical work spans flood simulation (FastFlood, LISEM, HEC-RAS), remote sensing analysis, probabilistic uncertainty modelling, and API development — but the thread running through all of it is the same: making outputs that people can actually use. Documentation, reproducibility, and clear communication are not afterthoughts for me; they are part of what it means to do the work properly.
